### Victor Ka Ming Fung

I build forecasting systems that turn weather and crop uncertainty into decisions a farm team can act on.

Lead Data and Climate Scientist, forecasting blueberry yield and weather risk for farms on four continents. Before that: data science consultant at McKinsey & Company, postdoc at MIT, and PhD at the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), where I modeled how farming practices change air quality and climate.

**Built and maintained**

- [ds-lead](https://github.com/kamingfung/ds-lead): 16 agent skills for data science work that ends in a decision. Frame the question, write and review pipeline code, check a number before you report it, and turn the result into decks, charts and one-page reports. See the [global food supply example](https://github.com/kamingfung/ds-lead/tree/main/examples/global-food-supply).
- [chase_offer_auto_enroll](https://github.com/kamingfung/chase_offer_auto_enroll): a browser extension with real users. It runs only on `chase.com` and sends no network requests.
