### Victor Ka Ming Fung

I build forecasting systems that turn weather and crop uncertainty into decisions a farm team can act on.

Lead Data Scientist at [Fruitist](https://fruitist.com), forecasting blueberry yield and weather risk for farms on four continents. Before that: data science at McKinsey & Company, a postdoc at MIT, and a PhD at CUHK modelling how farming practices change air quality and climate.
